Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the common ones.

Is TaskFlow free?

Yes. You can use every feature without paying and without an account.

Where is my data stored?

Guests: on your device only. Accounts: in your private TaskFlow account in the cloud, plus a copy on each device you use, so it syncs between your phone and computer. Only you can read it.

Will I lose my data if I clear my browser?

Yes, clearing site data removes it. Use Download my data on the app's Help page to keep a backup file.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the app opens without internet.

How do I install it?

Open the workspace, then use Install app in the sidebar or your browser's install option. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen. Only the workspace is installed.

Do I need an account?

No. You can try everything as a guest. Create a free account to get a profile photo, your own theme colour and sync between all your devices.
